Pricing questions

Straight answers

Why no setup fee?

Because the free draft does the selling. Spreading the build into the monthly plan keeps your risk at zero until you've seen — and approved — your actual website.

Is there a minimum term?

No long contract. The plan runs month to month; if you cancel, the site stays up until the end of the month you've paid for.

What counts as a "small edit"?

Text changes, price updates, new photos, hours, a new service added to an existing page — the everyday stuff. New pages and features are quoted as add-ons before we start.

How do I pay?

By a secure payment link when your site goes live, then monthly. You'll always know the exact amount — CA$99 — before anything is charged.

I already have a site. Same price?

Yes — taking over and managing an existing site is the same CA$99/month, starting with a fix-up pass (broken forms, HTTPS, stale details) so it earns its keep.
